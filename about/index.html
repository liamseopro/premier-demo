About Premier Heating & Cooling

Premier is a licensed, TSSA registered heating and cooling contractor in Strathroy, working for homeowners and home builders across the surrounding towns.

Doing the job right the first time

Premier Heating & Cooling has extensive experience in the heating and cooling trade, and the point of the company is straightforward: give every customer the best service we can, on the day we said we would be there.

We have installed a lot of systems over that time, and the work has earned us a reputation for getting the job done right the first time. That matters more in this trade than it sounds. A furnace sized wrong or a gas line run carelessly turns into somebody's cold January.

Our service is quick, professional and reliable, and much of our work comes from repeat customers and referrals. People call us back, and they pass our name to their neighbours, because of how our technicians handled the last job.

Licensed, registered and covered

  • Licensed and insured

    Licensed for the work we do and insured on every job we take on.

  • TSSA registered

    Registered with the Technical Standards and Safety Authority for gas fired equipment in Ontario.

  • WSIB covered

    Our people are covered by WSIB, so a job at your house never becomes your liability.

  • Lennox authorized dealer

    We install and repair Lennox equipment and can back it with the full Lennox warranty. We service all makes.

Builder HVAC

HVAC for new home construction

A large share of our work happens before anyone moves in. We work with home builders to size, rough in and finish complete heating and cooling systems, house after house, on the builder's schedule.

New construction leaves little room for a second attempt, because nobody wants to open a finished wall to correct a duct run. Getting it right the first time is why builders keep us on their list, and it is the same standard we bring to a service call on a house that has been lived in for 30 years.
