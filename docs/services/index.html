Air Conditioning

We install, repair and maintain central air conditioning across Strathroy, London and the surrounding towns. Every replacement starts with proper sizing for your home, and we walk you through the efficiency ratings so you can weigh the purchase price against what a higher SEER unit saves each summer.

Ductless Air Conditioning

Ductless mini-splits cool the rooms your ductwork misses: additions, upper floors that never cool down, converted garages and older homes built without ducts at all.

We install, repair and maintain single-zone and multi-zone systems, and we help you place each head unit where it will actually do the work. Most home installs are finished in a day.

Furnace

We install high-efficiency gas furnaces and repair and tune up all makes. Sizing matters as much as the efficiency number: an oversized furnace starts and stops constantly, wears out sooner and leaves rooms uneven.

Book a fall tune-up before the cold sets in, and call right away if the furnace will not start, will not stay lit, or runs without warming the house. Emergency service is available when it quits at the worst time.

Boiler

Boilers are their own trade, and we service and replace them regularly: systems feeding cast iron radiators, baseboards and in-floor heating alike.

Cold radiators, pressure that keeps dropping, or a system that needs bleeding every few weeks are all worth a service call now, before they turn into a no-heat call in January.

Water Heater

We repair and replace both tank and tankless water heaters. A standard tank costs less to install and is simple to service; a tankless unit costs more up front but delivers endless hot water and frees up floor space.

We will size either option to how much hot water your household actually uses and give you a straight recommendation. If your current tank is leaking, call today, because a failing tank rarely gives much warning.
