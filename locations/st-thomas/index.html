A city in the middle of a building boom

St. Thomas is in the middle of a construction surge. The Volkswagen PowerCo battery gigafactory is going up on the edge of the city, and new subdivisions are following across St. Thomas and Elgin County as builders work to house the people arriving with it.

That matters when you pick an HVAC company. A new build needs equipment sized and installed to a builder's schedule, and a young system needs its first service call done by people who know how these houses go together. Premier installs for home builders as well as homeowners, and St. Thomas is one of our core service areas. Our shop sits at 176 Metcalfe St E in Strathroy, about 40 minutes up the road.
